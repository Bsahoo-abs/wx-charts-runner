Sub: Deep Depression over Eastcentral and adjoining Southeast Bay of Bengal
[Cyclone Alert for Andhra Pradesh and adjoining South Odisha coasts—Orange
Message]
The Depression over Eastcentral and adjoining Southeast Bay of Bengal moved southwestwards with
speed of 5 kmph during past 6 hours, intensified into a deep Depression and lay centered at 1130 hours
IST of today, the 25th October, 2026 over the same region, near Latitude 15.1°N and Longitude 91.0°E,
about 420 km north-northwest of Sri Vijaya Puram (Andaman Islands), 870 km east-southeast of
Vishakhapatnam (Andhra Pradesh), 960 km east-southeast of Kakinada (Andhra Pradesh), 1060 km east-
Southeast of Machilipatnam (Andhra Pradesh) and 1130 km east of Bapatla (Andhra Pradesh).
It is very likely to move nearly southwestwards, and intensify further into a cyclonic storm over eastcentral
& adjoining southeast Bay of Bengal by 26th October early morning. Then it is very likely to move west-
northwestwards over the Eastcentral & adjoining Westcentral Bay of Bengal and intensify into a severe
cyclonic storm over westcentral Bay of Bengal around morning of 27th October, 2026.

is very likely to cross Andhra Pradesh & Yanam coasts between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam as a very
severe cyclonic storm with the maximum sustained wind speed of 115-125 kmph gusting to 140 kmph by
forenoon of 28th October, 2026.

Impact Expected over Andaman Islands on 25th -27th October
• Localized Flooding of roads, water logging in low lying areas and closure of underpasses mainly in
urban areas of the above region.
• Occasional reduction in visibility due to heavy rainfall.
• Disruption of traffic in major cities due to water logging in roads leading to increased travel time.
• Minor damage to kutcha roads.

October
• Total destruction of thatched houses/ extensive damage to kutcha houses. Some damage to pucca
houses. Potential threat from flying objects.
• Bending/ uprooting of power and communication poles.
• Breaking of tree branches, uprooting of large avenue trees.
• Widespread damage to standing crops, plantations, orchards, falling of green coconuts and
tearing of palm fronds. Blowing down of bushy trees like mango.
• Major damage to Kutcha and Pucca roads.
