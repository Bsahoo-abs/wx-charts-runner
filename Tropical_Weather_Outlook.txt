Sub: Deep Depression over Eastcentral and adjoining Southeast Bay of Bengal [Cyclone
Alert for Andhra Pradesh and adjoining South Odisha coasts.]
The Depression over Eastcentral and adjoining Southeast Bay of Bengal moved southwestwards
with speed of 5 kmph during past 6 hours, intensified into a Deep Depression and lay centered at
0600 UTC of today, the 25th October, 2026 over the same region, near Latitude 15.1°N and
Longitude 91.0°E, about 420 km north-northwest of Sri Vijaya Puram (Andaman Islands, 43333),
870 km east-southeast of Vishakhapatnam (Andhra Pradesh, 43149), 960 km east-southeast of
Kakinada (Andhra Pradesh, 43189), 1060 km east-Southeast of Machilipatnam (Andhra Pradesh,
43185) and 1130 km east of Bapatla (Andhra Pradesh, 43220).
It is very likely to move nearly southwestwards, and intensify further into a cyclonic storm over
Eastcentral & adjoining Southeast Bay of Bengal by 0000 UTC of 26th October. Then it is very
likely to move west-northwestwards over the Eastcentral & adjoining Westcentral Bay of Bengal
and intensify into a severe cyclonic storm over westcentral Bay of Bengal around 0000 UTC of
27th October, 2026.
Thereafter, it is very likely to move northwestwards, intensify further into a very severe cyclonic
storm and reach Westcentral Bay of Bengal near Andhra Pradesh coast around 0000 UTC of 28th
October, 2026. It is very likely to cross Andhra Pradesh & Yanam coasts between Bapatla and
Visakhapatnam as a very severe cyclonic storm with the maximum sustained wind speed of 115-
125 kmph gusting to 140 kmph by 0600 UTC of 28th October, 2026.

Remarks –
The guidance from various numerical models indicates that the Madden Julian Oscillation (MJO) is
currently in Phase 3 with amplitude more than 1. It is likely to continue in same phase during the
entire forecast period with amplitude more than 1 during next five days. Thus, MJO is favorable for
enhancement of convective activity and cyclogenesis over the Bay of Bengal (BoB) during next 7
days.
The guidance from the NCICS model indicates prevalence of westerly wind anomaly (7-9mps)
along with simultaneous occurrence of Equatorial Rossby wave (ERW), Kelvin wave (KW) &
Madden Julian Oscillation (MJO) wave over south and adjoining central BoB and easterly wind
anomaly (7-9 mps) over north BoB. These features indicate a highly favorable environment for
cyclogenesis (formation of depression) and its further intensification into a VSCS over the central
BoB till 27th Oct. Thereafter, MJO and ERW are likely to prevail over the region till 29th Oct and
may move away after that.
The Sea surface temperature is 30°C over eastcentral and adjoining Westcentral BoB and around
29°C along the east coast of India. However, due to the slow movement the ocean surface is
cooled and SST is slightly less 29 °C near system area. The ocean heat content is around 80-100
KJ/cm2 over most parts of Bay of Bengal with 100-120 KJ/cm2 over some pockets over southwest
and adjoining westcentral Bay of Bengal, North Andaman Sea, parts of southeast BoB, eastcentral
and adjoining northeast BoB.
The positive low-level vorticity is the same during past 6 hours and is around 100 X10-6 s-1 to the
southwest of system area with vertical extension upto 500 hPa compare to yesterday the
orientation of vorticity has changed from north-south to east-west, which suggests gradual
increase in westward component in movement. Low level convergence is around 05-10X10-6 s-1 to
the south of system centre. Upper-level divergence is 20 to the northwest of system centre and is
north-south oriented. Mid-level wind-shear (500-850 hpa) is moderate (5-10) over the system area

and along the predicted path, which is highly favorable for further intensification of the system.
However the deep layer wind-shear (200-850 hpa) is low to moderate (10-20 kt) over system area
upto 89°E, thereafter it is increasing slightly towards the west, along the expected path, which may
not be supportive for intensification. However, the deep layer wind- shear which is slightly
unfavorable will be offset by positive upper level divergence over the region.
The upper tropospheric ridge runs along 19 °N. System has come under the influence of upper
tropospheric easterly prevailing to the south of ridge and is therefore likely to be steered west-
northwestward on 25th Oct 2026. The ridge which was lying along 17° N yesterday has shifted
northwest along 19°N. It will support more westward component in movement.
A deep trough in westerly is seen along 65 °E to the north of 15 °N at 200 hpa level. It is expected
to reach 80°E on 28th. It would restrict the westward movement of the system after landfall and
would lead to nearly northwards re-curvature of the system.
The Total Precipitable water (TPW) indicates warm moist air (75mm) incursion into the system
area from south and southeast. Cold dry air incursion is also seen in the northwest sector which
may lead to slight weakening at the time of landfall. However the latest imagery shows warm moist
air availability along southwest center along Andhra Pradesh. The dry cold air over the Andhra,
Odisha and Chhattisgarh region will lead to rapid weakening of the system after landfall on 29th
when the system moves across these areas. Water Vapour imagery indicates strong poleward
outflow.
A favorable environment is prevailing for further intensification of the system (high SST, low wind
shear, simultaneous occurrence of MJO. ERW, KW, pole-ward outflow). The east – southeasterly
winds in the periphery of ridge located near 19° N would lead to west- northwestward movement
of the system till landfall and near northward movement under the influence of the trough in upper
tropospheric levels thereafter.
Most of the models are indicating initial southwards movement followed by nearly westward
movement towards Andhra Pradesh coast and almost northward movement after landfall. IMD
MME is also indicating similar movement. There is large divergence among various models with
respect to intensification. Peak intensity is varying between 50-70kt. Landfall time is varying
between 27/18 to 28/18 UTC.
Confidence Level in estimation of:
(a) Current location and intensity: High
(b) Forecast Track: Moderate to High
(c) Forecast intensity: Moderate to High
Considering all above, the deep depression over eastcentral adjoining southeast BOB is very likely
to intensify further into a cyclonic storm over the Eastcentral & adjoining Westcentral Bay of
Bengal by 0000 UTC of 26th October, and into a severe cyclonic storm over Westcentral Bay of
Bengal around 0000 UTC of 27th October 2026.
Thereafter, it is very likely to move northwestwards, intensify further into a very severe cyclonic
storm and reach Westcentral Bay of Bengal near Andhra Pradesh coast around 0000 UTC of 28th
October, 2026. It is very likely to cross Andhra Pradesh & Yanam coasts between Bapatla and
Visakhapatnam as a very severe cyclonic storm with the maximum sustained wind speed of 115-
125 kmph gusting to 140 kmph around 0600 UTC of 28th October 2026.
