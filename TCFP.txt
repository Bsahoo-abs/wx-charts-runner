Synoptic features (based on 0600 UTC analysis):
Depression over Eastcentral and adjoining Southeast Bay of Bengal [Cyclone Alert for
Andhra Pradesh and adjoining South Odisha coasts.]
The Depression over Eastcentral and adjoining Southeast Bay of Bengal remained
practically stationary during past 6 hours, and lay centered at 0300 UTC of today, the 25th
October, 2026 over the same region, near Latitude 15.3°N and Longitude 91.2°E, about 440
km north-northwest of Sri Vijaya Puram (43333, Andaman Islands), 980 km east-southeast of
Vishakhapatnam (43149, Andhra Pradesh), 980 km east south east of Kakinada (43189,
Andhra Pradesh), 1080 km east of Machilipatnam (43185, Andhra Pradesh) and 1150 km
east of Bapatla (Andhra Pradesh).
It is very likely to move nearly southwards, intensify into a Deep Depression and then
gradually recurve west-northwestwards over the Eastcentral & adjoining Southeast Bay of
Bengal today, the 25th October. Continuing to move west-northwestwards, it is very likely to
intensify further into a cyclonic storm over the Eastcentral & adjoining Westcentral Bay of
Bengal by 0000UTC of 26th October, and into a severe cyclonic storm over westcentral Bay
of Bengal around 0000UTC of 27th October, 2026.
Thereafter, it is very likely to move northwestwards, intensify further into a very severe
cyclonic storm and reach Westcentral Bay of Bengal near Andhra Pradesh coast around
0000UTC of 28th October, 2026. It is very likely to cross Andhra Pradesh& Yanam coasts
between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam as a very severe cyclonic storm with the maximum
sustained wind speed of 115-125 kmph gusting to140 kmph around 0600UTC of 28th
October, 2026.

M.J.O. Index:
The guidance from various numerical models indicates that the Madden Julian Oscillation
(MJO) is currently in Phase 3 with amplitude more than 1. It is likely to continue in same
phase during the entire forecast period with amplitude more than 1 during next five days.
Thus, MJO is favorable for enhancement of convective activity and cyclogenesis over the
Bay of Bengal (BoB) during next 7 days.
Equatorial waves guidance:
The guidance from the NCICS model indicates prevalence of westerly wind anomaly (7-
9mps) along with simultaneous occurrence of Equatorial Rossby wave (ERW), Kelvin wave
(KW) & Madden Julian Oscillation (MJO) wave over south and adjoining central BoB and
easterly wind anomaly (7-9 mps) over north BoB. These features indicate a highly favorable
environment for cyclogenesis (formation of depression) and its further intensification into a
VSCS over the central BoB till 27th Oct. Thereafter, MJO and ERW are likely to prevail over
the region till 29th Oct and may move away after that.
